# Third-party character artwork

The directory includes three character portraits from *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!*
(`超かぐや姫！`, distributed in Chinese as `超时空辉夜姬！`):

- `companion.png` depicts 月見ヤチヨ / Yachiyo Runami. The repository copy is a
  1080×1724 portrait export; its exact upstream file was not recorded when it was added.
- `iroha-sakayori.png` depicts 酒寄彩葉 / Iroha Sakayori. It was supplied by the
  Worldline operator for the built-in companion profile.
- `kaguya.png` depicts かぐや / Kaguya. It was supplied by the Worldline operator for
  the built-in companion profile.

Keep them only for an authorized Worldline build, and obtain permission before redistribution or derivative use. Official character reference: <https://www.cho-kaguyahime.com/>.

## User-supplied meme video

- `companion-memes/public/005.mp4` is the operator-supplied copy of the *Umamusume:
  Pretty Derby* fan meme “孤高曼波”. The work combines Matikanetannhauser/诗歌剧's
  “曼波” (ocean sunfish) chant with the mountaineering imagery associated with
  *The Climber* (`孤高之人`) and the melody of “La La La”. The widely circulated
  Bilibili upload is attributed to 一根华仔:
  <https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1JS421o7pM/>.

This video is bundled as a public Agent Vault reference resource at the operator's
request. It is not licensed under the repository's MIT License; all underlying
character, music, animation, and fan-work rights remain with their respective
rightsholders. Confirm redistribution permission before publishing a public build.
