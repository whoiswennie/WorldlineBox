# Worldlines, Su, and Huan

English | [简体中文](worldbuilding.zh.md)

Worldline Fantasy is not tied to a single setting. It is an entrance through which original worlds, characters, histories, and possible futures can be observed and connected. The shared creative foundation is the **Su (素) system**.

## Su

Su is the substrate from which worlds emerge. It can be understood through two inseparable aspects:

- As material possibility, it behaves like particles: it can gather, differentiate, and form structures.
- As awareness and relation, it behaves like waves: it can resonate, interfere, remember, and be observed.

Observation does not merely catalogue a finished reality. It helps possibilities acquire order. A world is therefore a temporarily stable pattern in a much larger sea of possible states, while change, assimilation, and evolution remain natural parts of its existence.

This is a creative model, not a claim about real-world physics. It gives stories a common grammar without forcing them to share one genre, era, cosmology, or outcome.

## Huan

Huan (幻) is an original character and the key observer linking the worldlines. She embodies possibility, variation, and the moment in which another path can still be taken. Her role is not to dominate every story or turn every world into the same plot. She can witness, cross, record, and sometimes influence a world while allowing its inhabitants and consequences to remain their own.

The white and dark forms associated with Huan express a paired movement: possibilities unfold, while formed things can also dissolve and return to the sea of Su. Neither movement is treated as a simple moral label.

> Every world has its story, and every story deserves to be observed. — Huan

## Relationship to the software

The narrative and technical layers reinforce one another without being confused:

- A runtime **profile** is a way of entering a particular worldline of capabilities.
- A **plugin** adds a bounded possibility without rewriting the whole system.
- A replayable **session event log** preserves what was observed and how the state changed.
- Host/Client boundaries let one world be projected through different interfaces.
- Huan provides the project's identity; Cordis provides the compositional discipline that keeps those possibilities coherent.

Future worldline material should remain original, compatible with the Su foundation, and open enough for stories with different aesthetics and rules. References used during design are inspiration only; this project does not present another franchise or setting as part of its canon.
